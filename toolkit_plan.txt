PERSONAL MINI-TOOLKIT PLAN

1. Number Guessing Game
The game asks the user to guess a secret number between 1 and 10 and gives hints until the answer is correct.
Concepts: variables, input, int conversion, while loop, conditionals, and f-strings.

2. To-Do List
The user can add tasks, view the current tasks, and remove a task by its number.
Concepts: lists, append(), pop(), for loop, conditionals, input, and f-strings.

3. Simple Calculator
The calculator asks for two numbers and an operation, then displays the answer or explains an invalid operation.
Concepts: variables, float conversion, conditionals, arithmetic, and f-strings.
